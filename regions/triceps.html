Triceps

The triceps run along the back of the upper arm and straighten the elbow — overuse from pressing movements is the most common source of tightness.

Common causes

  • Repetitive pushing/pressing motion
  • Overuse from triceps-focused training
  • Leaning on elbows for long periods
  • Overhead throwing or serving in sports
