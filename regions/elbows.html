Elbows

Elbow pain (often "tennis elbow" or "golfer's elbow") usually comes from repetitive gripping or wrist motion, not the elbow joint itself.

Common causes

  • Repetitive gripping (tools, racquet sports, typing/mousing)
  • Sudden increase in activity involving the forearm
  • Weak forearm/wrist muscles
