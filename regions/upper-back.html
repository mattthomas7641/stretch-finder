Upper Back

Upper back (thoracic spine) stiffness is extremely common with desk work — it's the area that gets stuck in a hunched position when we sit for hours.

Common causes

  • Prolonged sitting and slouching
  • Weak mid-back muscles relative to a tight chest
  • Carrying a heavy bag on one shoulder
  • Limited thoracic spine rotation/extension mobility
