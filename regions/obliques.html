Obliques

The obliques run along the sides of the abdomen and handle twisting and side-bending — they're prone to strain from sudden rotational movement.

Common causes

  • Rotational sports (golf, tennis, baseball, hockey)
  • A sudden or awkward twisting movement
  • Weak core stability
  • Side-bent sitting posture
