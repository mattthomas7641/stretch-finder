Quads (Front of Thigh)

Tight quads are common after running, cycling, or squatting, and can pull on the kneecap, contributing to knee discomfort.

Common causes

  • Running, cycling, or heavy squatting
  • Prolonged sitting with hips flexed
  • Muscle imbalance relative to the hamstrings
