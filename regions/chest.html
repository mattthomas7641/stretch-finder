Chest

Tight chest (pec) muscles are the flip side of upper-back stiffness — desk posture shortens the chest while it lengthens and weakens the upper back.

Common causes

  • Rounded-shoulder desk posture
  • Heavy pressing exercise without balancing pulling exercise
  • Shallow breathing / stress
