Lower Back

Lower back pain is one of the most common complaints and is frequently linked to tight hip flexors/hamstrings, weak core muscles, or too much sitting.

Common causes

  • Prolonged sitting compressing the lumbar spine
  • Tight hip flexors or hamstrings pulling on the pelvis
  • Weak core/glute muscles
  • Bending or lifting with poor form
