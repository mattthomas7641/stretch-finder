Adductors (Inner Thigh)

The adductors run along the inner thigh and pull the leg inward — they're a common site for strains ('groin pulls') in sports with quick lateral movement.

Common causes

  • Sudden lateral movement or direction changes (soccer, hockey, basketball)
  • Inadequate warm-up before sprinting or kicking
  • Muscle imbalance between adductors and abductors
  • Previous groin strain that healed short and tight
