Trapezius

The trapezius spans the back of the neck and upper back, and is where most people physically feel stress — it's usually the first thing to tense up.

Common causes

  • Stress, which concentrates tension in the upper traps
  • Hunched desk posture
  • Carrying a heavy bag or backpack on one shoulder
  • Sleeping without proper neck support
