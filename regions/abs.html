Abs (Rectus Abdominis)

The rectus abdominis runs down the front of the abdomen and can get tight from heavy ab training or achy from long stretches of sitting hunched forward.

Common causes

  • Overtraining abs without stretching afterward
  • Prolonged sitting hunched forward
  • Poor core endurance relative to other muscle groups
  • Sit-up/crunch-heavy workouts
