Shoulders

Shoulder pain often comes from overhead activity, poor posture pulling the shoulders forward, or impingement from repetitive motion.

Common causes

  • Rounded-shoulder posture from sitting/desk work
  • Overuse from throwing, lifting, or overhead sports
  • Rotator cuff irritation or impingement
  • Sleeping on the same side every night
